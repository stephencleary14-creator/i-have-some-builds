Resource 05 — Blog Management

How to Publish
Your Blog

A simple step-by-step guide to creating, formatting, and publishing articles to the SOC Fitness Playbook — no technical knowledge required.

Step 01

Log In to Contentful

All blog content is managed through Contentful — your content dashboard. You don't need to touch any code; everything is handled through a simple form interface.

1
Go to app.contentful.com
Open your browser and visit app.contentful.com. Log in with the email address registered for your SOC Fitness account.
2
Select your space
After logging in you'll land on your dashboard. Click the SOC Fitness space. If you only have one space it may open automatically.
3
Go to Content
In the left sidebar, click Content. This is where all your published and draft articles live.
Step 02

Create a New Article

Each article lives as a single entry in Contentful. Creating one takes about 5 minutes once your content is ready.

1
Click "Add Entry"
At the top right of the Content screen, click Add entry and select Blog Post from the dropdown. A blank entry form will open.
2
Fill in each field
Work through the fields listed in the reference table below. Required fields are marked — don't skip them or the article won't display correctly.
3
Publish
When everything looks right, click the green Publish button in the top right. The article goes live on your website immediately — no delay, no approval step.
Step 03

Field Reference

Here's exactly what each field does and what you should enter for each one.

Field What to Enter
Title Required The headline of your article. Keep it under 70 characters. Use plain language — avoid clickbait. Good example: "5 Recovery Habits Every GAA Athlete Should Build"
Slug Required The URL of the article. Use lowercase letters and hyphens only — no spaces, no punctuation. Example: 5-recovery-habits-gaa-athletes. Contentful may suggest one automatically based on your title — check it looks clean before publishing.
Excerpt Required A 1–2 sentence summary that appears on the blog index and homepage cards. Write it like a teaser — give enough to make someone want to click. Aim for 120–160 characters.
Body Required The full article content. Use the rich text editor — headings, bold, bullet points, and links are all supported. Write in plain paragraphs; you don't need to add any HTML.
Published Date Required Set this to the date you want the article to appear. Articles are sorted by this date, newest first. You can backdate or schedule ahead.
Category Required Pick one category from the list below. This controls where your article appears in the "Browse by Topic" section of the blog. Keep it consistent — don't create new categories unless discussed first.
Cover Image Optional The hero image for the article — see the image guide below. Strongly recommended. Articles without a cover image show a colour gradient fallback instead.
Good to know

You can save a draft at any time using Save without publishing. This lets you write an article over multiple sessions and only make it live when you're ready. Drafts never appear on the website.

Step 05

Cover Images

The cover image is the most impactful visual element of an article. It appears at the top of the article page, on the blog index cards, and on the website homepage. A good image makes your content look professional before anyone reads a word.

Ideal Size
1200 × 630px
  • Landscape / wide crop — never portrait
  • JPG format, under 2MB
  • Clear subject, not overly busy
  • Works without being cropped
Minimum Size
800 × 420px
  • Don't go smaller than this
  • Small images look blurry on desktop
  • Portrait images get cut off
  • Avoid screenshots at low resolution
Best Practice

The best performing images are wide, well-lit action shots — athletes training, coaching on the pitch, or in the gym. Avoid selfies, portrait photos, or anything with text overlaid (logos, Instagram watermarks). If you're taking photos specifically for the blog, shoot in landscape mode and aim for natural light.

How to upload an image in Contentful
1
Click the Cover Image field
In the entry form, scroll to the Cover Image field and click Add media or the + icon.
2
Upload a new asset
Choose Add new media → Upload from your computer. Select your image file (JPG recommended).
3
Add a title and publish the asset
Give the image a descriptive title (e.g. "Recovery session — GAA players"), then click Publish on the asset. You'll see it link back to your entry automatically.
Step 06

Writing Your Article

You don't need to be a professional writer. The SOC Fitness blog works because Shane knows his subject better than anyone else. These guidelines will help your content perform well and stay consistent.

✓
Write like you talk
Your athletes respond to you because you're direct and no-nonsense. Your blog should be the same. Short sentences. Plain language. No filler. If you wouldn't say it on the pitch, don't write it.
✓
Aim for 400–800 words
Short enough to read in 4 minutes. Long enough to be genuinely useful. Don't pad it out — if you've made your point in 400 words, stop there.
✓
Use headings to break up long articles
In the Body editor, use Heading 2 (H2) for section titles. This makes long articles scannable and also helps search engines understand your content.
✓
End with a call to action
Every article should finish with a clear next step for the reader — book a consultation, follow on Instagram, share with a teammate. Don't leave them hanging.
✓
Consistency beats volume
One solid article per week, published on the same day, will outperform three rushed articles published randomly. Pick a day — Tuesday or Thursday works well — and stick to it.
Before You Publish

Pre-Publish Checklist

Run through this list before hitting Publish on any article.

□
Title is clear and under 70 characters
□
Slug is lowercase with hyphens (no spaces)
□
Excerpt is 120–160 characters — a genuine teaser
□
Published Date is set correctly
□
Category is typed exactly from the approved list
□
Cover image is landscape, 1200×630px or close to it
□
Article ends with a clear call to action
□
Read it back once before publishing — fix any typos
After Publishing

Your article appears live on the website within seconds. Visit socfitness.com/blog to confirm it shows up. If you spot a mistake, go back to Contentful, edit the entry, and click Publish again — the update goes live immediately.
