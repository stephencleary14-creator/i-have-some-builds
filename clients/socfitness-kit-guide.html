Resource 06 — Email Marketing

Your Email List
(Kit)

How your subscriber list works, where to find it, and how to use it to grow SOC Fitness — explained simply.

How It Works

The Free PDF Does the Work

The GAA Fitness Primer is a free download on socfitness.com. When someone enters their email address to get it, two things happen automatically:

1
They receive a confirmation email
Kit sends them an email from shane@socfitness.com asking them to confirm. This keeps the list clean — only people who genuinely want to hear from you.
2
The PDF is delivered automatically
Once they confirm, Kit sends the GAA Fitness Primer straight to their inbox. You don't have to do anything — it's fully automatic.
3
They're added to your subscriber list
Their name and email are saved in Kit. They are now a contact you can email at any time — for free, with no ad spend.
Good to know

The list grows on its own every time someone visits the website and downloads the PDF. The more people who visit socfitness.com, the bigger your list gets — automatically.

Privacy & Compliance

SOC Fitness has a privacy policy in place at socfitness.com/privacy-policy. It covers how subscriber data is collected, stored, and used — and is compliant with UK GDPR and Irish data protection law. Anyone who signs up is doing so knowingly and can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email Kit sends.

Where to Find It

Viewing Your Subscribers

Your full subscriber list lives inside Kit. Here's how to get to it.

1
Log in to Kit
Go to app.kit.com and sign in with your SOC Fitness email address.
2
Click Grow → Subscribers
In the left sidebar, click Grow, then Subscribers. You'll see a list of everyone who has signed up — their email address, the date they joined, and whether they've confirmed.
3
Check the GAA Primer Download form
You can also click Grow → Landing Pages & Forms → select GAA Primer Download to see stats specific to that form — how many people have signed up and when.
What You Can Do

3 Ways to Use the List

Once people are on your list, you can reach them directly — no algorithm, no ad spend. Here are the three main things you can do.

1
Send a one-off email (Broadcast)
A broadcast is a single email sent to your whole list at once. Use this to announce something — a new programme, a seasonal offer, a training tip. Go to Send → Broadcasts → New Broadcast in Kit.
2
Set up an automated series (Sequence)
A sequence is a series of emails that goes out automatically, one after another, whenever someone signs up. You write them once and they run forever. Go to Automate → Sequences in Kit.
3
Target by interest (Tags)
As you add more sign-up forms in future (e.g. one for each programme), subscribers get tagged by what they're interested in. This lets you send an email only to people who expressed interest in a specific programme — much more relevant than emailing everyone.

The big picture

Every person on the list downloaded the PDF because they're interested in GAA fitness. They already know who Shane is. That makes this list far more valuable than a social media following — you own it, you control it, and it goes directly to their inbox without any algorithm getting in the way.
