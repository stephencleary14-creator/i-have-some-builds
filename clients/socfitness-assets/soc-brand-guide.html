01

Colour Palette

Gold / Amber
#F2A61D
Primary accent. Used for CTAs, one highlighted keyword per headline, stat figures, gold rule bars, and section label text. The defining colour of the brand — energy and achievement. Never use on more than one word per headline.
Black
#0D0D0D
Primary background. Communicates the intensity and professionalism of elite athletic training. Used for page backgrounds and the logo field.
Blue
#2F6DB5
Secondary accent. Drawn from the GAA kit colour in brand photography. Used for section labels on the website, status badges (e.g. "In Review"), icon accents, and secondary UI elements. Blue supports and frames — it never competes with Gold for attention.
White
#FFFFFF
Primary text on dark backgrounds. Used for body copy, nav links, and secondary outlined buttons. Never use on light backgrounds.
Dark Grey
#1A1A1A
Section backgrounds, card surfaces, and nav backdrops. Provides depth and layering against the primary black without jarring contrast.
Mid Grey
#333333
Card backgrounds, dividers, and UI surfaces. The middle tier of the dark palette — used for interactive elements and hover states.
Colour Usage Ratio & Role Rules

60% Black / Dark Grey — backgrounds and page canvas.
30% White — primary text, icons, and supporting elements.
10% Gold — accent, CTA, and brand highlights only. Never overuse gold — its power comes from restraint.

Gold role: Headlines (one highlighted word only), CTAs, stat figures, gold rule bars, section label text, template top bar strip.
Blue role: Website section labels, UI status badges, icon accents, secondary navigation elements. Blue supports; it never leads.

Logo Usage

Two Logo Files
SOC_logo.png — Transparent
White wordmark + shield on a transparent background. Used in the website navigation bar, social media template overlays, and anywhere the background colour provides sufficient contrast. Requires a dark (or solid colour) background behind it.
SOC_logoRef.png — Black Field
Same mark on a solid black rectangle. Used for print documents, PDF exports, email headers, and any context where a transparent background cannot be guaranteed (e.g. white paper). The black field ensures consistent rendering across all outputs.
Primary — Transparent on Black
SOC_logo.png. Preferred application for all digital use.
Transparent on Dark Grey
SOC_logo.png. Acceptable on any dark background.
Black Field on Light Backgrounds
SOC_logoRef.png. Use when background is white or light.
Transparent on Brand Blue
SOC_logo.png. Use sparingly — social headers, kit contexts.
Clear space = ½ logo height on all sides

Clear Space & Sizing Rules

  • — Always maintain a minimum clear zone of half the logo height on all four sides.
  • — Minimum digital size: 80px tall. Never render smaller — the stripe detail and lettering will degrade.
  • — Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, or apply drop shadows to the logo.
  • — Do not place on a busy photograph without a sufficient dark overlay (minimum 50% black).

Brand Voice

Uncom­promis­ing
We don't soften the message. High standards are non-negotiable — in training and in how we communicate.
Athletic
Every word earns its place. The voice is lean and purposeful — no waffle, no filler. Copy that moves like an athlete.
Irish
Rooted in GAA culture. The tone is familiar, direct, and proud — the language of the pitch and the dressing room.
Expert
We know GAA performance inside out. The voice is confident and credible — backed by results, not empty claims.
Gritty
We celebrate hard work and honest effort. The voice is real — no motivational poster clichés, just earned conviction.

Do Say

  • Train Like an Athlete
  • Built for GAA Performance
  • Earn your edge in the gym
  • Strength that shows up on match day
  • No fluff. Just results.
  • Your position. Your programme. Your season.

Don't Say

  • Transform your body in 30 days
  • Are you beach-body ready?
  • Smash your goals
  • Fitness journey begins here
  • Level up your lifestyle
  • Feel amazing and look great

Photography

On-Pitch Athletic Performance
Coaching in Action — Gym Environment
⚡
Real Athletes, Real Moments
No stock photography. Every image shows real GAA athletes in genuine training or match scenarios. Authenticity is non-negotiable.
🏟
GAA Context
Photography should anchor the brand in GAA. Pitches, club kits, Croke Park — the visual world of the GAA player.
🎞
Cinematic & Moody
Dark treatment, high contrast, gritty feel. Avoid bright, clean, wellness-brand aesthetics. This is performance, not lifestyle.
💪
Show the Work
Images should capture effort — a barbell under load, a sprint, a tackle. Show the training, not the result photo.
📐
Dark Overlay on Backgrounds
When photos are used as backgrounds (hero, section dividers), apply a minimum 50% black overlay to ensure text legibility and brand consistency.
🎨
Brand Colour Alignment
Preferred photography features athletes in blue and gold — the SOC kit colours. This reinforces brand coherence across all touchpoints.

Template Types
Base Templates
Logo-only. Three position variants per format — A (logo top-left), B (logo top-right / top-centre), C (logo bottom-left / bottom). Used as blank branded canvases for photography overlays or content where the visual is the hero.
Headline Templates
Four content variants per format — Client Spotlight, Training Tip, Match Day, New Post. Large headline in Barlow Condensed 900, one keyword highlighted in Gold. Designed for regular social content cadence.
Headline Variants — Content Types
CLIENT
SPOTLIGHT
Athlete testimonials and results posts. Gold on "CLIENT" — the key achievement word.
TRAINING
TIP
Quick coaching advice, technique cues, weekly focus areas. Gold on "TIP" — the value word.
MATCH
DAY
Game-day motivation, match recaps, performance highlights. Gold on "MATCH" — the event word.
NEW
POST
Blog announcements, new content drops, programme launches. Gold on "NEW" — the attention word.
Universal Design Rules
Background — Black Only
All templates use #0D0D0D as background. Dark grey (#1A1A1A) is reserved for website sections and cards — never for social template backgrounds.
Gold — One Word Only
Gold highlights exactly one complete keyword per headline. Never mid-word (no splitting across a line break). Never the final word of a negative phrase. Gold on the word that carries the most meaning.
Top Bar — Always Present
A 10px gold bar anchored to the top edge appears on every template, base and headline. It is the primary brand anchor — the first element a viewer reads as SOC Fitness.
Logo — Within Safe Zones
Feed: 28px minimum inset from all edges. Stories: logo must sit below the 200px top UI zone and above the 300px bottom UI zone. Instagram's own UI must never overlap the logo.
No Additional Text
Headline templates carry the logo and the headline — nothing else. No "SOC FITNESS" wordmark repeated in type (the logo handles that), no straplines, no URLs on the template itself.
File — Transparent Logo
Always use SOC_logo.png (transparent) on social media templates. The black background provides the contrast; the black-field version (SOC_logoRef.png) would create a visible rectangle on the template.
