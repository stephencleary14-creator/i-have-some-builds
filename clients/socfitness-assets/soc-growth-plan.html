Online Growth Plan
02
First 90 Days
QUICK WINS
Five actions with the biggest impact in the shortest time. Most of these Shane can action himself — no developer needed.
1
Set Up Google Business Profile
Week 1 — Highest Priority
Create and verify a free profile at business.google.com. This puts SOC Fitness on Google Maps and in local search results. Set the category to “Personal Trainer”, add service area (Derry, Strabane, Limavady, Coleraine), write a description using the phrase “GAA personal trainer in Derry”, and upload photos of Shane training clients. Post a weekly update to stay active in the algorithm.
2
Collect 5 Google Reviews
Weeks 1–4
Contact existing clients directly and ask for a Google review. Five genuine reviews can move SOC Fitness into the local map pack — the three results that appear above everything else in a local search. Ask clients to mention “GAA”, “Derry”, or a specific result they achieved (e.g. “improved my speed before county championship”).
3
Launch Website with 3 Core Pages
Weeks 1–3
The three pages that matter most at launch: Homepage (mention Derry and Northern Ireland naturally), Services page (separate sections for 1:1 PT, Online Coaching, Small Group PT), and an About page with Shane’s name, story, and credentials. These three pages are the minimum SEO foundation before any other content is worth creating.
4
Publish First 2 Blog Posts
Weeks 2–6
Two posts should go live as soon as possible: “The 12-Week GAA Pre-Season Strength Plan for Club Players” and “How Many Days a Week Should a GAA Club Player Train in the Gym?” These are most likely to rank quickly and be cited by AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. Full blog plan on the next page.
5
List on 4 Key Directories
Weeks 2–4
Submit SOC Fitness to Bark.com, Yell.com, DistinguishedTeaching.co.uk, and StarOfService.co.uk. Use the exact same business name, address, and phone number on each. Takes one afternoon and builds the early search credibility Google looks for in a new business.
⚠
Timing Note GAA players search most for training plans and coaches between October and January — the pre-season window. Getting the website and Google Business Profile live before September means SOC Fitness can capture that demand in year one.
