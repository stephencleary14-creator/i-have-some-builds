Programme Overview

What's
Included

The Online Coaching Programme is designed for people who want structure, accountability and expert guidance, but need the flexibility to train around their own schedule. Whether your goal is to build strength, improve fitness, lose body fat, gain confidence in the gym or simply follow a more focused plan, online coaching gives you the support and direction to stay consistent.

You will receive a personalised training programme built around your goals, experience, equipment access and lifestyle. Your workouts will be clearly laid out so you know exactly what to do each session, with progression built in to help you keep improving over time.

Alongside your training, you'll have weekly check-ins, support and guidance to help keep you accountable and make adjustments where needed. This allows the programme to fit around real life while still giving you the structure needed to move towards your goals.

  • Personalised training programme
  • Access to your own app to track progress and complete check-ins
  • Weekly check-ins and ongoing accountability
  • Support with training, nutrition and lifestyle habits
  • Programme adjustments based on progress and feedback
  • Suitable for gym-based training or training around your available equipment
  • Ideal for beginners and regular gym-goers who want more structure
