About SOC Fitness

Train Like a
GAA Athlete

I'm Shane O'Connor, a strength and conditioning coach from Derry City. I studied Sport & Exercise Science at university, completed my PT qualification, and I'm now studying an MSc in Performance Coaching.

I've coached across club and county setups, from underage to senior.

BSc Sport & Exercise Science
PT Certified
MSc Performance Coaching, Setanta College
Derry City FC S&C Placement
Derry GAA S&C Internship (U-15 & 16)
S&C Coach, underage & senior men's teams

I've always had a strong interest in physical fitness and how gym work can improve performance on the pitch. While no exercise is ever truly sport-specific, I focus on choosing movements that give GAA players the best carryover to their game and the best return for their effort.

I don't believe results come from training seven days a week, they come from keeping training simple and trying to improve every session.

Train smarter
to perform better.
