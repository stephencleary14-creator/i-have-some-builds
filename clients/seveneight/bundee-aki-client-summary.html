The story exists. The infrastructure to carry it does not.

Bundee has six active brand partnerships and a compelling personal narrative, but 55% of his posts are paid ads and his personal voice is almost entirely absent. This is an activation problem, not a brand problem.

The Outsider story is his most powerful asset and the most underused.

Outsider scored highest in the research, near-unanimous. A New Zealand-born Samoan who became an Irish international and Lions tourist is a genuinely distinctive life story. It has never been told in his own voice on his own channels.

Family man is the financial services unlock. The market has already validated it.

Family values is the only association where the full FS decision chain agreed positively in the research. The commercial market has already tested the brief: Avonmore, Samsung, and IKEA have all paid for it. The Avonmore reel hit 932,000 plays. Financial services is the natural next step.

He is the most credible multicultural bridge in Irish sport. No brand has activated it.

Samoan roots, New Zealand upbringing, Irish international: his dual heritage resonates with first-generation Irish audiences in a way no other Irish rugby figure can replicate. The opportunity is unoccupied.

The discipline incident has a clock attached to it.

Board-friendly scored lowest of all ten associations. An 18 to 24 month window of clean public record is the practical minimum before a serious FS conversation becomes realistic. That clock is already running. It shapes which categories to pursue first, not whether to pursue commercial activity at all.

The Strategic Approach

Two tracks running simultaneously: commercial now, brand building in parallel

Track 1 — Commercial (now)

Own the Fearless Outsider space in the energy and performance category

Fearless and Outsider rated highest in the research, near-unanimous across all persona types. Both map naturally onto the energy and performance category. The World Cup 2027 cycle is a live activation window. Brands in this space (Lucozade Sport, Kinetica, Myprotein, Optimum Nutrition) are planning ambassador rosters now. The brief writes itself: a centre who came from outside the expected pathway and refused to leave.

Based on prototype research only. Category priorities and specific brand targets must be confirmed with management before any outreach begins.
Track 2 — Brand Building (in parallel, starting now)

Build the brand codes now. The financial services window opens in 18 to 24 months.

Track 2 runs alongside Track 1 from day one, not after it. Financial services is the highest-value category available to a family-facing Irish personality, and the family man positioning is already commercially proven: Avonmore, Samsung, and IKEA have all paid for it. The discipline clock sets the timeline: 18 to 24 months of clean public record before a boardroom conversation becomes realistic. The content built for Track 1 today is the evidence base an FS buyer will scroll through in 2028.

Recommended Actions

Eight tactics, in priority order : owned content, earned media, community, and commercial

1
Start now — time-sensitive

The Outsider Series — Chapter 1: Samoa

The Samoa visit is a live news hook that will fade. Build Chapter 1 now: a long-form Instagram carousel in Bundee's own words, his own photographs, one clear narrative thread. The full series follows the arc: Samoa, Galway arrival, Lions call-up, Baby Aine. It is the most powerful asset in his commercial portfolio and entirely absent from his channels.

2
Start now

Social content infrastructure — three times per week

The most cited commercial blocker in the research. Bundee has six active brand deals, 208,000 followers, and almost no personal voice between the ads. Three post types per week: Performance (match-day, training), Family and Community (unforced personal content), Identity (Outsider Series, heritage, values). No dead periods between rugby news cycles.

3
This week

PR and editorial — place the Samoa story now

Pitch specific story angles to sports desks while the news peg is live: the Samoa reconnection, the Outsider who became definitively Irish, the Lions test played the day his wife gave birth in a car park. Primary targets: Irish Times, Sunday Times Sport, Irish Examiner, Off The Ball.

4
Ongoing

Community — local roots and Pacific Island heritage

Bundee has been in Galway for over a decade. That should be visible. Youth rugby visits, school appearances, and local club connections build the community proof that makes the family man positioning credible, and they cost almost nothing to document and publish. Alongside that, visible engagement with the Pacific Island community in Ireland is a distinct angle no other Irish rugby figure can claim. Rooted in Connacht, proud of where he came from: these two threads are the foundation the financial services positioning needs.

5
Next 30 days

TikTok launch

Bundee has almost no TikTok presence. Fan-created content generated 49,809 views in one month without his involvement. The Judeinthehat reel (14,505 likes, no paid support) proves the format: Bundee unscripted is more compelling than Bundee scripted. Two posts per week. Short, warm, direct to camera. Launch before the Connacht season starts.

6
Next 60 days

Podcast appearances

Bundee is a strong podcast guest: warm, candid, a life story that is compelling audio. Before the first appearance, he needs a fixed four-minute narrative: New Zealand, Samoa, Galway, Ireland, Lions, five kids, faith. Target shows: Off The Ball, Second Captains, The High Performance podcast. One well-matched appearance creates more reach than months of social content.

7
Next 90 days

RTÉ documentary pitch

Pitch RTÉ Sport on a short documentary built around the Outsider arc. The Lions tour and Baby Aine story combined with the Samoa visit creates a natural two-act structure. This story is broadcast-ready in a way few Irish rugby personalities can offer. The Samoa visit makes the pitch timely now.

8
Next 90 days

Anchor partnership case study

Structure the next commercial partnership to produce a publishable case study: content metrics captured, campaign documented, brief summary published at the end of the contract. One case study with real results removes the biggest single objection every future brand buyer will raise. Avonmore may already be a candidate if the relationship allows it.
