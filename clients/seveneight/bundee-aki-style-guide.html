02 — Brand Essence

Who Is Bundee

Bundee Aki is one of the most compelling stories in modern rugby. Born in Otahuhu, South Auckland, to Samoan parents, he grew up in Manurewa — a tight-knit Pacific community where family, faith, and sport were everything. A natural across every sport, he was remembered at school as a "lovable rogue" with an outstandingly beautiful personality who hated being confined by four walls.

He came to Ireland in 2014, recruited by Pat Lam to join a Connacht side few expected to win anything. Within two seasons, he was player of the Pro12 tournament as Connacht lifted the title for the first time in their history. Galway adopted him. He adopted Galway. In the little town of Oranmore, locals named a pub booth after him — the Bundee Booth — and he became as much a part of the West's identity as the Burren or the Claddagh.

By 2017 he was eligible for Ireland. By 2018 he was a Grand Slam winner and part of the team that beat the All Blacks — the country he came from. In 2021 he toured with the Lions. In 2022, Andy Farrell made him captain against the Maori All Blacks in New Zealand. He choked up when he spoke about it. In 2025, aged 35, he became a two-time Lion — missing the birth of his fifth child to play in the first Test, and starting the series-winning match in Melbourne.

Off the pitch, he is known as an energiser — the player who changes the mood of any room. A devoted father of five, a keynote speaker on diversity and inclusion, a man who carries his Samoan roots and his Irish life with equal pride. His Instagram bio reads simply: "Everything that I have is by the Grace of God."

Belonging Without Borders
Bundee's identity is plural and proudly so — Samoan roots, New Zealand upbringing, Irish heart. He doesn't dilute any of them. He shows that belonging is something you build, not something you're handed. This duality — the outsider who became the beating heart of a community — is the most powerful thing about his brand.
Force & Warmth
On the pitch he is a wrecking ball — a 102kg inside centre who runs straight, tackles hard, and breaks gainlines through sheer physicality. Off it he is warm, funny, emotional, and generous. The contrast is not a contradiction — it is precisely what makes him compelling. Power without aggression. Intensity without coldness.
Sacrifice & Honour
The story of missing his fifth child's birth to take the field for the Lions captures everything about how Aki approaches duty. He plays every game with the understanding that the jersey means something — to his family, to the province, to the nation. Honour is not abstract for him. It is a daily decision.
Faith, Family, Community
His Instagram bio says it plainly: everything he has is by the grace of God. His Samoan upbringing centred on extended family and community — an ethos he brought wholesale to Galway. Five children. A pub booth named after him. A speaker platform built on inclusion. His community is always the frame.
03 — Colour Palette

Colour

The palette draws from three anchors: the deep greens of Connacht and the Irish West; the warm Pacific gold of Samoa and South Auckland; and the Lions red that marks the pinnacle of rugby honour. Together they tell Bundee's story without a single word.

04 — Typography

Typography

Syne carries force and personality without defaulting to the condensed sports cliché. Its geometric letterforms have character — particularly at display weight — while Inter provides clean, readable support at body size. Together they position Bundee's brand as modern and distinctive, built for a career that extends beyond the pitch.

05 — Brand Voice

Voice & Tone

01
Direct & Unfiltered
Bundee speaks plainly. No PR polish, no hedging. When he is proud, you hear it. When something moves him, he shows it. His voice has the directness of someone raised where words matter because actions matter more. It is the opposite of corporate. It sounds like a man, not a brand.
Do "Didn't get the result we wanted. We know it. The work continues Monday. Always."
Avoid "Following today's performance, we remain committed to our collective journey of continuous improvement."
02
Warm & Community-Led
Every post, every interview, every public moment references other people — his family, his teammates, his province, his community. Bundee does not position himself alone. He is always inside a story that is bigger than himself. This generosity of spirit is one of his most distinguishing brand qualities.
Do "To every person in Galway who's made my family feel at home — this one's for you. Always."
Avoid "Honoured to have delivered for the team and achieved this personal milestone."
03
Grounded in Faith & Gratitude
His Samoan upbringing, his Christian faith, and his own improbable journey mean that gratitude is not a social media trope for Bundee — it is a lived orientation. The acknowledgement that luck, grace, and the people around him have shaped everything he has is always near the surface. This gives his voice a depth that most athlete accounts lack.
Do "Everything I have is by the grace of God. Every single day I get on that pitch, I know how lucky I am."
Avoid "Hard work pays off. #Grind #NoExcuses #EarnIt"

06 — Visual Direction

Art Direction

01 Core Principle
Always Grounded
Bundee's power comes from being rooted — in the turf, in Galway, in his family, in his heritage. Visuals should feel low to the ground. Low camera angles amplify his physicality. No float, no artifice. Every image should look like it could have been taken by someone who was actually there.
02 Core Principle
Real Moments Over Staged Shots
The most powerful Bundee imagery is emotional and unguarded — choking up when he speaks about captaining Ireland in New Zealand; the look on his face after a test win; a quiet moment with his family. Seek documentary truthfulness over polished production. Authenticity is the product.
03 Core Principle
Community Always in Frame
Even in solo portraiture, the community should be implied — through environment (Galway, Connacht Park, Samoan imagery), through context, or through the direct gaze that says "I am doing this for more than myself." Bundee is never alone in his story, and the imagery should reflect that.
ACTION SHOT Low angle · High intensity · Gainline break · Motion blur on tacklers
PORTRAIT — JERSEY OFF Environmental · Galway / Connacht location · Natural light · Quiet authority
COMMUNITY MOMENT Bundee with kids / fans / teammates · Unguarded · Warm light · Real emotion
HERITAGE REFERENCE Samoan/Pacific cultural context · Family setting · Warm tones · Authentic
LIONS / PRESTIGE MOMENT Dark, high-contrast · Red accent · Lions jersey · Weight and honour
POST-MATCH TUNNEL Raw, candid · Sweat and jersey · Unpolished · Human, not heroic
Photography — Seek
  • Low angles that emphasise physicality and presence
  • Available / natural light — warm golden tones where possible
  • Environmental portraits that place him in Galway or Connacht
  • Candid family and community moments, unposed
  • Post-match honesty — sweat, emotion, jersey loosened
  • Cultural markers: Samoan colour, Pacific warmth, Irish green
  • Eye contact that is direct and confident, never confrontational
Photography — Avoid
  • High-gloss studio setups that feel removed from reality
  • Aggressive or purely intimidating poses — power with warmth always
  • Imagery that erases his Pacific identity or cultural background
  • Overly retouched or stylised edits — authenticity over aesthetics
  • Lone-wolf hero framing — always community-adjacent
  • Cold, desaturated colour grading — this brand runs warm
07 — Logo & Mark

Identity Concepts

Wordmark Concept Primary Identity
BUNDEE AKI

BUNDEE in white Syne ExtraBold at maximum weight. AKI in Pacific Sun gold at two-thirds the x-height, tracked wide. A gold rule bar below BUNDEE anchors the mark and references the accent colour. The surname subordinate but present — the nickname is the brand.

Symbol Mark Icon / Monogram

A shield form references Irish rugby heritage and the warrior tradition of Pacific Islander culture. The B initial in white Syne ExtraBold sits on a deep Connacht Forest green. Three Pacific Sun gold dots at the crest nod to Samoan tatau motifs. The gold arc at the base suggests both the Claddagh and the Pacific horizon. Concept only — requires type rendering in production.
