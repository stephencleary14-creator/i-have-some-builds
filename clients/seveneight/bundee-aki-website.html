Auckland Born.
Ireland Made.

Bundee Aki grew up in Auckland, New Zealand, shaped by Samoan heritage, family, and the unforgiving demands of Southern Hemisphere rugby. He crossed the world to join Connacht in 2014, and what began as a professional move became something far deeper.

"I love playing for Ireland, I love the people in Ireland. They've given nothing but love. I can only show it back with the way I play and the way I represent the country."

After qualifying for Ireland through residency, Aki became one of the most trusted centres in the game. Physical, direct, and relentless with ball in hand. His selection for the British and Irish Lions in 2025 placed him among the elite of northern hemisphere rugby.

Off the pitch, Bundee is known for his warmth, his commitment to Connacht's community, and the cultural pride he brings to everything he does. He is a father, a leader, and a symbol of what it means to belong.

The Journey

2014
Arrives at Connacht
Signs with Connacht Rugby, beginning a journey that would redefine his life and career.
2016
PRO12 Champions
Part of the historic Connacht side that claimed their first-ever PRO12 title. A moment that defined a province.
2017
Ireland Debut
Qualifies through residency and earns his first Ireland cap. The first of many appearances in green.
2018
Grand Slam & All Blacks
Part of the Ireland side that claimed the Six Nations Grand Slam and beat the All Blacks — the country he came from.
2019
Rugby World Cup, Japan
Selected for Ireland's World Cup squad, cementing his place as one of the country's most important backs.
2025
British & Irish Lions
Selected for the Lions tour. The pinnacle of northern hemisphere rugby, and recognition of sustained excellence.

What
He Stands For

Bundee Aki's impact stretches far beyond the rugby field. From nurturing youth talent in the west of Ireland to championing cultural identity, his values are woven into everything he does.

The Next Generation

Bundee is a passionate advocate for grassroots rugby in Connacht and beyond. Visiting schools, coaching clinics, and showing young players what's possible.

Roots & Pride

Samoan heritage runs deep. Bundee carries his roots with pride, bridging two cultures and showing that identity is never a boundary. It is a bridge.

Built on Family

Family is the foundation. A devoted father and husband, Bundee's warmth and loyalty off the pitch mirrors the commitment he shows every Saturday.
