The stephenjcleary Brand Style Guide

I wanted to build a blog and a few tools, but before any of that I needed to figure out what they'd look like. So I started with a brand style guide — a single HTML file that defines the colours, typography, and visual rules for everything I make.

I'm a big Liverpool FC fan, and I wanted to base the design on a shirt I love. This shirt is the one.

Robbie Fowler, Liverpool Away Kit 1996/97

Mohamed Salah, Liverpool Away Kit 2025/26

The palette I built from it uses those warm creams as the base, with a deep red lifted from the Liverpool crest as the accent colour. Paired with a near-black for text and a warm brown-grey for secondary labels.

Here's how each one gets used:

#C8102E — deep red, the primary accent. Links, highlights, anything that needs to draw the eye.
#A00E24 — a darker red for hover states and emphasis.
#EDE0C4 — warm ecru, the main background. Straight from the shirt.
#F7F2EA — lighter cream for cards and section backgrounds.
#1C1C1C — near-black for all body text.
#5C5247 — warm brown-grey for secondary text and labels.

The typeface is Outfit. Warm and readable without feeling corporate. Headlines use Title Case. Body copy uses sentence case. Nothing in all-caps, ever.

Six colours, one font, a handful of rules. It's simple enough to apply consistently across anything I build — including this blog.
