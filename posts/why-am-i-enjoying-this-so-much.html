Why am I enjoying this so much?

I've been rewatching One Tree Hill on Netflix, like so many others apparently. There's an episode in Season 5 where Nathan and Haley are in couples therapy. The therapist asks them why they don't describe themselves the way they used to — a basketball player and a musician. Their answer is simple: life got in the way.

It's a throwaway moment in a teen drama. But it stuck with me.

My career started with making things. My undergrad was Multimedia Design — I was building websites regularly, learning by doing, putting things out into the world. My big break at adidas came because I took initiative. I made a campaign to find an internship: built a website, networked, documented the whole thing as it happened. It won a Digital Advertising Northern Ireland award.

That instinct — to make something rather than just talk about it — is what got me in the room. And somewhere along the way, I stopped exercising it.

I think that's why throwing myself into Claude Code has felt so good. It's not just that the tools are impressive or the builds are useful. It's that I'm making things again. Properly making them — from an idea to something you can open in a browser and use.

One small thing that captures this well: I built a skill this week just to stay on top of Claude news. One command — /NewClaudeFeatures — and it goes and finds everything that's dropped recently, then explains it back to me in plain language. No jargon, no press release tone. Just: here's what's new and here's what it means.

I used to describe myself as someone who builds things. Life got in the way for a while. It's good to be back.
