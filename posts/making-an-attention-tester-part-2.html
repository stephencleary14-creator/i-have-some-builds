Making an Attention Tester: Part 2

Part 1 got the core working — upload a video, get a graph, toggle between platforms. Part 2 was about pushing it further.

The first addition was connecting to Gemini. Once that was in place, the tool could actually read and analyse the video content. The result is an AI headline at the top — a one-line summary of what the video contains. It sounds simple but it changes how the tool feels. It goes from a graph to something that understands what it's looking at.

That got me thinking. If it can analyse a video, can it tell me whether the brand shows up in it? I added a Distinctive Brand Asset checker. You upload an image of your logo, character, or any visual brand asset, and the tool confirms whether it appears in the video — and at what second.

The natural next step was to connect those two things. The DBA moment is now marked on the attention graph as a line — so you can see not just when the brand appears, but how much attention the platform is likely to be giving at that exact moment. In this case, the Lotto logo appears at 6 seconds on TikTok, right around where average active attention starts to drop off.

That combination — attention curve, platform toggle, and DBA marker — is what makes it useful. It's not just a graph. It's a question: is your brand showing up at the right moment?
