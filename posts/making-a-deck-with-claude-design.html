Making a deck with Claude Design

Claude Design is a new feature that lets you create polished visual presentations directly inside Claude. I wanted to try it properly.

The brief was simple: make a short how-to deck for the Attention Tester. Step by step, explain the features, include visuals of the tool. Here's the exact prompt I gave it:

"I want to make a short deck which explains how to use the Attention Tester tool. It should be a simple step by step which explains the features and benefits. Include visuals of the tool."

I uploaded two things before writing the prompt: my stephenjcleary brand style guide, and the Attention Tester project folder. That's it. No further instruction on colours, layout, or structure.

What came back was an 8-slide deck. Dark opening and closing slides using my near-black background, red accent colour, and STEPHENJCLEARY wordmark. Step slides using the deep red left panel with cream text, and the actual tool UI on the right. The chart colours — dark bars, sand passive, red active — were pulled directly from the tool itself.

It got the brand right without being told to. The type hierarchy, the way the step numbers are labelled, the use of white space — all consistent with the style guide I'd uploaded.

1 / 8

I've spent hours upon hours of my working life on decks. It's really nice knowing that time could be saved in future and put into making better work.

Not every output will be this clean on the first pass. But this one was. Worth sharing.
