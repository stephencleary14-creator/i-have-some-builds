He kicked! Animating my Brand Mascot

The original figurine on this blog was made in ChatGPT. One session, a good prompt, and it came out better than expected — the CLEARY base, the SJC crest, the kit colours. I was happy with it. I understand the value in characters as Distinctive Brand Assets. They offer flexibility in creative — they can do dialogue, or do interesting things. But they work best when used consistently.

The problem is ChatGPT has no memory of a character. Every time you go back, the face changes. It's stateless. That made the figurine a one-off rather than an asset I could actually use.

So I wanted to fix that. I wanted a version I could reproduce — the same face, the same kit, any pose.

The answer was a LoRA. A LoRA is a fine-tuned AI model trained on a specific set of images. Instead of prompting a general model and hoping it lands on your face, you train a small model that knows what you look like. Then you use that every time.

I did this on fal.ai, using around 15 photos of myself. The result:

That's a generated image, not a photo. The face is locked — I can reproduce it any time I want with a prompt.

From there I prompted the model to render that face as a figurine — chibi proportions, cream jersey, number 9, SJC crest, CLEARY base. Lots of iteration. Lots of prompts.
